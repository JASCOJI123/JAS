Our Story

Where heritage meets modern hospitality

THE PLOV was born from a simple promise: preserve the soul of Uzbek national cuisine and serve it with pride. In the heart of Bukhara, our kitchen honors recipes handed down through generations while welcoming today's travelers with comfort and elegance.

We choose fresh ingredients daily, simmer with patience, and plate with intention—because authenticity is not a trend; it is our identity.
